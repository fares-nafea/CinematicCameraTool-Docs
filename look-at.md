# Look At

> Turn the camera to face a part, model or attachment.

**LOOK AT** turns the viewport camera to face a target once.

## Steps

1. In Studio, select a **part, model or attachment**.
2. In **LOOK AT**, press **Pick Target**.
3. Press **Look At**. The camera turns to face the target.

The panel shows the chosen target ("Target: ..."). You can pick another target at any time.

## Good to know

- Look At is a one-time turn. It does not keep following the target (use [Follow](follow.md) or [Rig](rig.md) for that).
- It changes the viewport camera in Studio only. It is not part of the timeline, a recording or the MP4.
- It is blocked while a Preview runs ("Stop the preview first.").

## Common mistakes

- **"Pick a target first.":** press Pick Target after selecting something.
- **"Target is too close to camera":** move the camera away from the target.
- **"Target unavailable":** the target was deleted. Pick another one.
