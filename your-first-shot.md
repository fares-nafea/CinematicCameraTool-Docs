# Your first shot

> Set a Start and End camera and play your first Preview.

A new scene already has one empty shot. This guide turns it into your first cinematic move.

## Set the cameras

1. In Roblox Studio, move the viewport camera to where the shot should **begin**.
2. In the Cinematic Camera Tool panel, press **Set Start Camera**.
3. Move the camera to where the shot should **end**.
4. Press **Set End Camera**.

The panel shows the saved cameras. You can also move the Start and End markers that appear in the viewport with Studio's own tools; the shot follows them.

## Choose the duration and easing

- **Duration:** how long the move takes. The default is 3 seconds (0.1 to 600 seconds).
- **Easing:** how the speed changes. The default is SineInOut.

Available easings: Linear, SineInOut, QuadInOut, CubicInOut and QuartInOut. **Linear** keeps a constant speed. The *InOut* easings start and finish slowly.

## Preview

Press **Preview**. The camera plays the move and then returns exactly where it was. Press **Stop Preview** to stop early.

## Common mistakes

- **Preview does nothing or shows a message:** the shot needs both a Start and an End camera.
- **You cannot edit while it plays:** editing is blocked during a Preview. You will see "Stop the preview first."
