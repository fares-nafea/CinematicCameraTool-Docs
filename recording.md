# Recording

> Record the camera while a Preview plays and keep it as a take.

**RECORDING** captures the camera while a Preview plays. The result is called a **take**.

## Steps

1. Choose the **FPS**: 24, 30 or 60. The default is 30.
2. Press **Record**. It plays the Preview from the start and records the camera.
3. The recording ends when the Preview ends, or press **Stop Recording** to end it early. The Preview keeps playing.
4. The status shows **Take ready**.

## Record Again and Clear

- **Record Again** asks for a second click before it replaces the take.
- **Clear** removes the take.

## Good to know

- A take lives in memory only. It is **temporary until you export it**, and it is lost when the plugin reloads.
- The longest take is 600 seconds.
- While recording, editing and the live tools are blocked ("Stop recording first.").
- You do not need a take to export an MP4. The MP4 export uses your shots directly.

## Common mistakes

- **Expecting the take to be saved automatically:** export it (see [Export camera data](export-camera-data.md)).
- **Changing shots during a recording:** it is blocked. Stop the recording first.
