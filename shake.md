# Shake

> A quick camera shake with intensity, frequency and duration.

**SHAKE** shakes the camera for a short time.

## Steps

1. Set **Intensity**, **Frequency** and **Duration**.
2. Press **Start Shake**. Press it again to stop early.

## Settings

- **Intensity:** 0 to 10. The default is 0.5.
- **Frequency:** 1 to 60 variations per second. The default is 12.
- **Duration:** 0.05 to 30 seconds. The default is 0.5.

You can change the values while the shake is running.

## Good to know

- Shake is a **live tool**: one at a time, a Preview stops it, and the camera returns to where it was afterwards.
- It is not baked into shots, recordings or the MP4.
- **Shake** and [Advanced Shake](advanced-shake.md) are separate systems and do not run at the same time.

## Common mistakes

- **Nothing seems to happen:** raise the intensity, or lengthen the duration.
- **The shake is too jittery:** lower the frequency.
